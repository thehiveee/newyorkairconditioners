This policy explains what information New York Air Conditioners ("we", "us") collects through this website and how we use it.

Who we are

New York Air Conditioners, 141 W 28th St #8400, New York, NY 10001. Phone (646) 692-0033, email contact@nyc-airconditioner.com.

What we collect

  • Call-back requests. When you use the “Request a call back” form we receive your name, phone number, the service you chose and any message you add. The form is delivered to us by email through a form-processing service (FormSubmit).
  • Calls and emails. If you call or email us, we receive your phone number or email address and whatever you tell us.
  • Website usage. We use Vercel Web Analytics to count page views and visits in aggregate. It does not use cookies and does not identify you personally.
  • Optional analytics cookies. If we enable Google Analytics, it only runs after you click “Accept” on the cookie notice. You can decline and the site works the same.
  • Browser storage. We store a small flag in your browser's session storage so the discount pop-up appears only once per visit, and your cookie choice in local storage. These are not shared with anyone.

How we use it

  • To call you back, give quotes, schedule and carry out service, and keep service records.
  • To understand which pages are useful and improve the website.
  • To meet legal, tax and insurance obligations.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with providers that help us run the website and our business — our website host (Vercel), our form-processing service (FormSubmit), Google (fonts and, if you accept, analytics) — and where the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep call-back requests and service records for as long as we need them to serve you and to meet our legal and warranty obligations, then delete them.

Your choices

You can ask us to show, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you by contacting us using the details above. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under your state's privacy laws; we will honor requests as the law requires.

Children

This website is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Security

The site is served only over encrypted HTTPS connections. No method of transmission is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
